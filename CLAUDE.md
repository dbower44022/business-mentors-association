## What this repository is

The organizational records of the Business Mentors Association: its rulings, its open questions, and the documents that follow from them. No application code lives here.

## Read first

`README.md`, then `DECISIONS.md`. Do not propose anything that contradicts a ruling without saying plainly that it does.

## The rules

- **A ruling is Doug's.** Only Doug makes one, and it is recorded in `DECISIONS.md` with its date and in plain words. Anything Claude decided or drafted is marked as such, never mixed in with the rulings.
- **When a ruling changes, say what it replaces.** The old ruling stays visible, marked as replaced, with the date. Nothing is quietly dropped.
- **This repository is the only live copy.** Nothing about the Association is kept in a chat, a Claude project or a downloads folder. A copy that cannot track its source is how a confident wrong answer gets made.
- **Every document carries a header and a change log.** Document, Version, Status, Owner, and Last Updated in `MM-DD-YY HH:MM` format, with a change log table at the end.
- **Write for a technical but not expert reader.** No record identifiers standing in for content, no shortened terms, no jargon used as if it were a definition. Plain words, short sentences.
- **Commit messages follow Conventional Commits** (`docs:`, `fix:`, and so on).

## The standards this follows

Doug's own standards — the operating modes, the writing register, terminology precision and reply format — live on his wiki and are mirrored in his `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`. They are not copied here. Read them there; a copy in this file would go stale.
