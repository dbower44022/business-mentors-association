These are not tasks, and none has a recommendation attached here. They are recorded so they are asked rather than rediscovered. A question leaves this file by being ruled in `DECISIONS.md`.

## The company and its tax treatment

1. **Whether the company seeks tax-exempt status** (rulings 5 and 23). A limited liability company is not exempt automatically. The tax authorities allow an exempt limited liability company only under set conditions, one of which is that every owner is itself exempt. An attorney should confirm this before anything is filed.
2. **Whether fiscal sponsorship still has a part to play** (ruling 5). Sponsorship suits a project rather than a company, so the original plan of sponsorship first may simply fall away.
3. **Who owns the company** (ruling 23). The proposal says the member chapters own it, which is Claude's draft, not a ruling.
4. **Whether the nonprofit pricing survives** (ruling 21). Anthropic's discount, Google's free edition and EspoCRM's nonprofit price each depend on qualifying, and a company may not. About $1,500 a year to the Association, and far more to each chapter.
5. **How the shield is kept** — separate accounts, separate records, separate decisions. A shield is maintained, not just formed, and the governing documents should say how.
6. **The state of formation and the filing fees.** The one-time costs in the cost list are a nonprofit's; a company's differ.

## Money

7. **The size bands, their shares and the dues amounts** (ruling 11). What makes a chapter small, medium or large is not defined.
8. **Whether Cleveland Business Mentors' development work so far counts toward its dues.**
9. **What happens when a chapter does not pay.** The chapter network's ruling is that service stops and access never does; the notice period and wind-down are not written.
10. **Reimbursement for bills paid personally** before the Association can pay its own (ruling 18).
11. **Insurance the company needs** — in particular cyber liability, which is not priced. Association volunteers can reach chapters' systems, which hold client and mentor records. A company's cover is not the same as a nonprofit's.

## Governance

12. **The membership number that turns the technical committee into an elected one** (ruling 10).
13. **The committee's decision rule** — majority, or agreement (ruling 10).
14. **Whether the technical lead can refuse a request on security or cost grounds** (ruling 10).
15. **Whether the double majority also applies to committee elections and to admitting members** (ruling 12).
16. **Whether a member can be removed, and how.**
17. **Whether Cleveland Business Mentors has any special standing as a founding member.**
18. **How a chapter proposes a local change for adoption into the standard** (ruling 3).
19. **What happens to a defect a chapter reports after the release vote has passed** (rulings 2 and 4).

## Software and licensing

20. **Which open-source licence the chapter applications carry** (ruling 6).
21. **Checking the repository's whole history for passwords, keys and Cleveland's data** before it is made public (ruling 6).
22. **The written boundary between CRM Builder and the chapter applications** (ruling 6).
23. **A contributor agreement** for volunteers who write code.
24. **Whether the Association's test system can share Cleveland's Advanced Pack licence**, or needs its own (rulings 15 and 21).
25. **The price of the Google Integration extension licence**, which the standard requires on every chapter's CRM.

## Operations

26. **Whether the shared test system is rebuilt or moved** (ruling 15).
27. **What Cleveland uses as its own test system afterwards** (ruling 15).
28. **Who the second account owner is** (ruling 18). Five setup steps wait on this.
29. **Which people get paid mailboxes and which addresses are shared groups** (ruling 20).
30. **The real cost of the test system** — the server, application and database. The current figures are estimates; Cleveland's bill is the better source.
31. **The exit rehearsal** — an owner and a date. The chapter network's own note is that an unexecuted exit path is a promise, not a capability.

## Agreements

32. **The chapter agreement.** A draft exists and is in review elsewhere. It was written under the old rulings, so it likely contradicts rulings 1 to 4, and it now has to say what the company shields the chapter from.
33. **The attorney's review and its cost** — the company's formation and operating agreement, the chapter agreement, and the open-source licence.
34. **The Association's legal name**, and whether the working name and the domain match it.
