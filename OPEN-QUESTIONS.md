# Open questions

**Document:** What is not yet decided about the Association, and what each question waits on
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Current
**Owner:** Doug Bower
**Last Updated:** 09-25-26

---

These are not tasks, and none has a recommendation attached here. They are recorded so they are asked rather than rediscovered. A question leaves this file by being ruled in `DECISIONS.md`.

## Legal and money

1. **Who the fiscal sponsor is.** Everything about the Association's money waits on this, including paying any bill (ruling 18).
2. **Whether a fiscally sponsored program qualifies for the nonprofit programs** from Anthropic, Google and EspoCRM. The difference is roughly $1,500 a year to the Association, and far more to each chapter (ruling 21).
3. **The size bands, their shares and the dues amounts** (ruling 11). What makes a chapter small, medium or large is not defined.
4. **Whether Cleveland Business Mentors' development work so far counts toward its dues.**
5. **What happens when a chapter does not pay.** The chapter network's ruling is that service stops and access never does; the notice period and wind-down are not written.
6. **Reimbursement for bills paid personally** before the sponsor is in place (ruling 18).

## Governance

7. **The membership number that turns the technical committee into an elected one** (ruling 10).
8. **The committee's decision rule** — majority, or agreement (ruling 10).
9. **Whether the technical lead can refuse a request on security or cost grounds** (ruling 10).
10. **Whether the double majority also applies to committee elections and to admitting members** (ruling 12).
11. **Whether a member can be removed, and how.**
12. **Whether Cleveland Business Mentors has any special standing as a founding member.**
13. **How a chapter proposes a local change for adoption into the standard** (ruling 3).
14. **What happens to a defect a chapter reports after the release vote has passed** (rulings 2 and 4).

## Software and licensing

15. **Which open-source licence the chapter applications carry** (ruling 6).
16. **Checking the repository's whole history for passwords, keys and Cleveland's data** before it is made public (ruling 6).
17. **The written boundary between CRM Builder and the chapter applications** (ruling 6).
18. **A contributor agreement** for volunteers who write code.
19. **Whether the Association's test system can share Cleveland's Advanced Pack licence**, or needs its own (rulings 15 and 21).
20. **The price of the Google Integration extension licence**, which the standard requires on every chapter's CRM.

## Operations

21. **Whether the shared test system is rebuilt or moved** (ruling 15).
22. **What Cleveland uses as its own test system afterwards** (ruling 15).
23. **Who the second account owner is** (ruling 18). Five setup steps wait on this.
24. **Which people get paid mailboxes and which addresses are shared groups** (ruling 20).
25. **The real cost of the test system** — the server, application and database. The current figures are estimates; Cleveland's bill is the better source.
26. **Cyber liability insurance**, which is not priced. Association volunteers can reach chapters' systems, which hold client and mentor records.
27. **The exit rehearsal** — an owner and a date. The chapter network's own note is that an unexecuted exit path is a promise, not a capability.

## Agreements

28. **The chapter agreement.** A draft exists and is in review elsewhere. It was written under the old rulings, so it likely contradicts rulings 1 to 4.
29. **The nonprofit attorney's review and its cost** — the sponsorship agreement, the bylaws, the chapter agreement and the open-source licence.
30. **The Association's legal name**, and whether the working name and the domain match it.
