# Client Code of Conduct — model draft

**Document:** Model code of conduct for clients, partners and sponsors of a chapter
**Version:** 0.1
**Status:** Draft — drafted by Claude, not legal advice. Every chapter reviews and adapts it before publishing (Association ruling 49).
**Owner:** Doug Bower
**Last Updated:** 11-08-26 10:15

---

Mentoring works when both sides can count on each other. By asking for a mentor, or working with CHAPTER-NAME as a partner or sponsor, you agree to the following.

1. **Be honest.** Give accurate information about yourself and your business.
2. **Be respectful.** Treat mentors, staff and other participants with courtesy. Harassment or discrimination of any kind ends the relationship.
3. **Keep appointments.** Come prepared, and give at least a day's notice if you need to cancel.
4. **No payment or gifts.** Mentoring is free. Do not pay, hire or give gifts of value to your mentor, and do not ask your mentor to invest in, lend to or work for your business.
5. **Decisions are yours.** Your mentor shares experience, not professional advice. Take legal, accounting, tax or financial questions to a licensed professional.
6. **Respect confidentiality.** What your mentor shares about other clients or their own work stays private, as what you share stays private with us.
7. **Raise concerns with us.** If something is not working, or a mentor does anything that troubles you, write to CONTACT-EMAIL.

CHAPTER-NAME may end mentoring with anyone who does not follow this code.
