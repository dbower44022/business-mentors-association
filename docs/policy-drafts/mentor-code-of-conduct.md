# Mentor Code of Conduct — model draft

**Document:** Model code of conduct for a chapter's volunteer mentors (the guide's "Mentor Code of Ethics")
**Version:** 0.1
**Status:** Draft — drafted by Claude, not legal advice. Every chapter reviews and adapts it before publishing (Association ruling 49).
**Owner:** Doug Bower
**Last Updated:** 11-08-26 10:15

---

As a volunteer mentor with CHAPTER-NAME, I agree to the following.

1. **Confidentiality.** I keep everything a client shares with me confidential, during mentoring and after it ends. I discuss a client only with CHAPTER-NAME's staff and co-mentors, for the purpose of the mentoring.
2. **No compensation.** I accept no payment, fee, commission, equity or gift of value from a client, and I do not sell my own or anyone else's services to a client.
3. **Conflicts of interest.** I tell CHAPTER-NAME at once of any interest I have in a client's business, competitors or suppliers, and I step aside if asked.
4. **Within my role.** I share my experience. I do not give legal, accounting, tax, financial or investment advice as a mentor, and I suggest a licensed professional when a client needs one.
5. **Respect.** I treat every client with courtesy and without discrimination, whatever their background, and I never harass or pressure a client.
6. **Records.** I keep notes of my sessions in CHAPTER-NAME's applications, not in personal files, and I use my CHAPTER-NAME email address for mentoring.
7. **Information security.** I protect client information, use two-step sign-in on my CHAPTER-NAME accounts, and report any loss or exposure at once.
8. **Reporting.** I tell CHAPTER-NAME promptly about any concern for a client's safety or any breach of this code.
9. **Ending well.** If I cannot continue with a client, I tell CHAPTER-NAME so the client can be supported by another mentor.

CHAPTER-NAME may end a mentor's volunteering for a breach of this code.
