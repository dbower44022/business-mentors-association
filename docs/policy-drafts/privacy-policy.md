## Who we are

CHAPTER-NAME ("we") offers free business mentoring by volunteer mentors. This policy explains what personal information we collect through our website and our mentoring programme, why, where it is kept, and the choices you have. Questions go to CONTACT-EMAIL.

## What we collect

- **When you ask for a mentor:** your name, email address, telephone number, your business's name, address and details, and what you would like help with.
- **When you apply to be a mentor:** your name, contact details, professional background, areas of experience and, if you choose to send one, your résumé.
- **When you ask a question, apply as a partner or sponsor, or register for an event:** your name, contact details, your organization, and what you tell us in the form.
- **When you sign up for our newsletter:** your name and email address, and your consent to receive it.
- **When you donate:** your name, email address and the amount. Your card details go to our payment processor, Stripe, and we never see them.
- **When you work with a mentor:** notes of your sessions, and email between you and your mentor, which is copied onto your record.

## How we use it

To match you with a mentor and support your mentoring; to answer your questions; to run our events and send you what you registered for; to send the newsletter you asked for; to thank you for and record donations; and to understand, in total, how our programme is doing. We do not sell your information.

## Where it is kept, and who helps us

- Our customer relationship management system, run on our own server, holds your record.
- Our applications keep a copy of each form you submit, in their own database.
- Google Workspace carries our email and stores documents you share with us.
- Zoom runs our online events and tells us who attended.
- Constant Contact sends our newsletter to those who signed up.
- Stripe processes donations.
- Cloudflare provides our website's domain services and counts visits (see below).

Each of these services handles your information only to provide its service to us. [The attorney names any further processors and where the data is stored.]

Your mentor, and the staff who arrange mentoring, can see your record. Mentors are volunteers bound by our Mentor Code of Conduct, which requires them to keep what you share confidential.

## Our website

- **Visitor statistics.** We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and does not identify you.
- **Campaign marker.** If you arrive through one of our campaign links, the website keeps a small marker in your browser tab so that a form you fill in on the same visit credits that campaign. It holds nothing personal and is gone when you close the tab.
- **Forms.** Our forms appear inside our web pages but are served by our own applications, at APP-ADDRESS.

We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

## Your choices

You may ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, and you may unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in every issue. Write to CONTACT-EMAIL. [The attorney adds any rights required by the law that applies to the chapter.]

## Children

Our programme is for adults running or starting a business. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

## Security

We limit access to the people who need it, require two-step sign-in on our accounts, and use secure connections for our website and applications. No system is perfectly secure; if something goes wrong that affects you, we will tell you.

We will post any change here, with the date it took effect.
